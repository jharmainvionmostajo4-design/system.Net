# Website Research

## Project Overview
Sweet Bliss Dessert Boutique is an online dessert shop website designed to display different desserts and provide customers with a convenient way to browse products.

## Purpose of the Website
The purpose of the website is to showcase dessert products, display their prices, and allow customers to add products to a shopping cart.

## Target Audience
The website is intended for people who enjoy desserts, cakes, pastries, and other sweet treats.

## Design and Layout
The website uses a sweet and welcoming theme. It includes a homepage, product categories, product listings, an About Us section, store information, customer reviews, and contact details.

## Main Features
- Product search
- Dessert categories
- Product descriptions and prices
- Shopping cart
- Store information
- Contact details

## Research Notes
The website content and features were reviewed based on the Sweet Bliss Dessert Boutique website.
